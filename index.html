Before the demo

Do I need training to use it?

No. When you don't know how to do something, ask Sofia. She answers in English, Spanish or Portuguese and gives you the steps on the screen you're on.

Does it work for my trade?

Yes. You set the stages for each kind of work, like rough-in and trim, interior and exterior, or your own, and costs follow those stages. Builders use the same system for the whole job.

Do I need a computer?

No. It runs in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. The office can use a computer at the same time.

Does Sofia change my numbers?

No. Sofia is read-only. She reads your jobs, invoices, spending and stock to answer you, and every entry is still made by you.

Can Sofia enter an invoice for me?

Not yet. She tells you where to go and what to fill in, and you review the invoice and save it.

How do my subs use it?

You open a job to a sub, and they see only that job in their portal. They post photos, update progress by milestone and send invoices, which wait for your approval.

How does getting started work, and what does it cost?

We're starting with a pilot group of contractors and subs. On the demo we look at how you run jobs today and agree on how to start. Pricing hasn't been set yet.

Where does my data go, and who sees it?

Sofia reads your data only to answer your questions, and she can't change it. Subs see only the jobs you open to them. Our written privacy and security policy isn't published yet; ask us about it on the demo and we'll go over how your data is stored.
